About Me

Passionate developer with a keen eye for simulation-driven software systems

Hello! I'm a Software and Simulation Engineer based in Singapore with a passion for creating engaging digital experiences. I specialize in interactive simulation and engineering-focused software systems. My background spans software engineering, simulation development, and robotics.

I'm constantly learning and staying up to date with the latest tech news, AI advances in technology and best practices.

Outside of software development, I stay active through table tennis and running, which help me reset and think clearly after long debugging sessions or complex problem solving. During my free time, I enjoy playing video games, especially those that require strategic thinking and quick reflexes.

During my time in National Service, I served as a Naval Diver (Frogman), an experience that shaped my discipline, resilience, and ability to stay calm under pressure, qualities I carry into my engineering work today.

Woodlands Checkpoint VR Trainer

A VR vehicle-search trainer set in a digital twin of Woodlands Checkpoint. Trainees search cars with fully interactive doors, bonnets and compartments, then record and classify every find on an in-hand tablet for scoring. Instructors run the live checkpoint: barrier gates, traffic flow, weather, and scenario injects such as contraband cars, nervous drivers and document mismatches.

Redhill Peaks Studio

A 3D interior design studio that runs entirely in the browser, built to plan the renovation of a 4-room HDB BTO flat. Trace the floor plan, hack and build walls, furnish rooms with one-click layouts, try materials and real sunlight, then walk through the result in first person. It produces a budget, shopping list, electrical plan and HDB renovation checks ready to hand to a contractor.

React Three.js TypeScript

Astro Cow

Astro Cow is a cozy puzzle game set in space. You pilot a small UFO, jumping between planets to rescue stranded animals and deliver them safely to the target planet marked with a red flag. Each planet has its own gravity, so movement, timing, and positioning matter more than speed. Plan your jumps carefully, avoid drifting into danger, and figure out the best order to move animals without losing them to the void.
